# 學校限制手機，學生比較好嗎？（Goodyear 等人, 2025）

> 原標題：School phone policies and their association with mental wellbeing, phone use, and social media use (SMART Schools): a cross-sectional observational study
> 出處：The Lancet Regional Health – Europe。頁碼用期刊頁碼（和 PDF 頁碼相同）。

## 一句話摘要

比較英國「限制手機」和「允許手機」的中學後發現：限制手機的學校，學生**在校時**用手機的時間比較少，但**整天的總使用時間**、心理健康、睡眠、成績和課堂行為**都沒有差別**。

## 研究問題

學校限制學生在校使用手機，能不能減少整體使用時間、改善心理健康和其他表現？

## 研究設計與對象

- 【相關性研究｜英國 12–15 歲中學生 N=1227】這是橫斷面觀察研究（cross-sectional observational study），比較 30 所英格蘭中學（[[goodyear-2025]], p. 1: "We conducted a cross-sectional observational study with adolescents from 30 English secondary schools"），共招募 1227 名學生（[[goodyear-2025]], p. 1: "We recruited 1227 participants (age 12–15) across 30 schools"）
- **限制型學校**：上課日不能為了娛樂使用手機（[[goodyear-2025]], p. 4: "In restrictive schools, phones were not allowed to be used during the school day for recreational purposes"）
- **允許型學校**：任何時間或特定時間可以用手機（[[goodyear-2025]], p. 5: "In permissive schools, phones were permitted to be used at any time or at certain times"）
- 學校**不是**隨機分配的，研究者是利用各校原本就不同的政策來比較。所以這是觀察研究，不是實驗。見 [[correlation-vs-causation]]。
- 主要結果指標是心理幸福感（mental wellbeing），用 WEMWBS 量表測量（[[goodyear-2025]], p. 5: "The primary outcome was mental wellbeing, measured using the Warwick–Edinburgh Mental Well-Being Scale"）
- 次要結果指標包括焦慮、憂鬱、運動、睡眠、課堂行為、成績和問題性社群媒體使用（[[goodyear-2025]], p. 5: "Secondary health and education-related outcomes assessed in pupil participants were: anxiety, depression, physical activity, sleep, classroom behaviour, attainment, and problematic social media use"）。**沒有測量注意力或專注力。**

## 主要發現 A：限制手機 vs. 允許手機

1. **在校使用時間比較少。** 限制型學校的學生，在校用手機的時間少了 0.67 小時、用社群媒體的時間少了 0.54 小時（[[goodyear-2025]], p. 7: "Students attending restrictive schools had significantly lower in school smartphone time"）
2. **但調整其他因素後，整體使用時間沒有顯著差別。** 平日、週末和一整週的使用時間，限制型學校的中位數其實比較高（[[goodyear-2025]], p. 7: "the median use duration in restrictive schools was greater"），但在調整其他因素後的模型中，差異都不顯著（[[goodyear-2025]], p. 7: "in the adjusted models, the differences were non-significant"）
3. **心理幸福感沒有差別**（[[goodyear-2025]], p. 7: "with no evidence of a difference between groups when controlling for other variables"）；焦慮和憂鬱也沒有差別（[[goodyear-2025]], p. 7: "there was no evidence of a significant difference between permissive and restrictive schools"）
4. **睡眠、運動、成績、課堂行為也都沒有差別**（[[goodyear-2025]], p. 7: "No significant differences in pupil outcomes were observed between permissive and restrictive schools for all other behavioural outcomes"）
5. **就算是「手機完全拿不到」的學校也一樣。** 只看手機必須鎖起來或交出去的學校，和允許型學校比較，結果仍然沒有差別（[[goodyear-2025]], p. 7: "In the sensitivity analysis using only restrictive schools where phones were inaccessible to pupils, there were no significant differences with permissive schools across all outcomes"）
6. **作者的解釋：在校使用只占總使用量的一小部分**（[[goodyear-2025]], p. 12: "phone and social media use is only a small contributor to overall use on school days"）
7. **作者的結論：目前沒有證據支持現行形式的學校禁手機政策**（[[goodyear-2025]], p. 1: "The findings do not provide evidence to support the use of school policies that prohibit phone use during the school day in their current form"），這類政策需要進一步發展（[[goodyear-2025]], p. 12: "indicate that these policies require further development"）

## 主要發現 B：使用時間和各項結果的關聯

> 這部分是**相關**，作者明確表示不能排除反向因果（見「限制」）。

- 使用時間越長，心理幸福感越低（[[goodyear-2025]], p. 8: "Increases in smartphone and social media time were associated with reduced mental wellbeing on a weekday, on a weekend day, and across a week"）
- 使用時間越長，焦慮、憂鬱和問題性社群媒體使用越高（[[goodyear-2025]], p. 8: "Increases in smartphone and social media time were associated with increases in anxiety, depression and problematic social media use across all measurement periods"）
- 使用時間越長，睡眠越差（[[goodyear-2025]], p. 9: "Increased smartphone and social media time was associated with reduced sleep efficiency and sleep duration"），英文和數學成績也越差（[[goodyear-2025]], p. 9: "Increased smartphone and social media time was also associated with poorer English and Maths attainment"）

## 這代表什麼

> 以下是本 wiki 的解讀，不是論文的結論。

- 學校限制手機，確實讓學生在**學校裡**少用一點；但學生回家後的使用量沒有因此減少，所以整體來看差別不大。
- 「學校禁手機沒有用」說得太快了。比較準確的說法是：**目前這種形式的政策，沒有觀察到對心理健康、睡眠、成績和行為的差別。**
- 這篇沒有測量注意力，所以**不能**用它來回答「學校禁手機能不能讓學生上課更專心」。
- 「使用時間越長，各方面越差」是相關。也可能是原本就比較不快樂、睡不好的孩子用手機比較多。

## 限制

**作者自述：**
- 橫斷面設計很難推論因果，也不能排除反向因果（[[goodyear-2025]], p. 12: "the study design is cross sectional which makes it difficult to draw conclusions about causality and reverse causality cannot be ruled out"）
- 有選擇偏誤和未測量到的干擾因素的風險（[[goodyear-2025]], p. 12: "there is a risk of selection bias and unmeasured confounding in this study"）
- 使用時間是學生自評的，限制型學校的學生可能會少報在校使用時間（[[goodyear-2025]], p. 12: "pupils in schools with restrictive phone policies may have been more likely to under-report their"）

**wiki 補充：**
- 只在英國做，其他國家的學校文化和政策可能不同。
- 限制型學校大多只是要求「手機關機放在書包裡」（[[goodyear-2025]], p. 7: "the majority of schools in the restrictive category required phones to be kept off in school bags during the school day"），手機完全拿不到的學校只有少數幾所，作者也說這是小樣本的分析（[[goodyear-2025]], p. 6: "we performed a sensitivity analysis with a small sample"）。所以「嚴格禁止」的情況證據比較少。
- 沒有測量注意力和課堂專注度。
